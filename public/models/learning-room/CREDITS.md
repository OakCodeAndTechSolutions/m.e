[Objaverse](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse) mirror of Sketchfab.
Each original sits under `real/`; the runtime copy under `optimized/real/` is resized
and repacked by `scripts/optimize-room-assets.mjs` with geometry unchanged.

The 1-gang switch, cooktop isolator and rotary dimmer are generated by
`scripts/generate-learning-room-models.mjs` (`switch.glb`, `isolator.glb`,
`dimmer.glb`): extruded C2000-style plates on PVC wall boxes, in white PC matched to
the Type I double outlet. No CC-BY Australian C2000 rocker or rotary dimmer exists.
The wall lights and framed prints use procedural geometry.

## Blender house assets

The island, dining table and chair, coffee table, media console, pendant,
potted gum, switchboard door, RCBO and isolator moulds, moving grips, and test
plunger under `optimized/house/` are original Blender meshes. The modelled
proportions, materials and details were informed by these Australian photographs
and product images.

The sofa uses the attributed Modern Couch mesh by Smitty__Werben listed above,
reupholstered and exported through Blender with CC0 photographic wool maps.
The oak floor is a Blender surface using the photographed Wood Floor maps.

- Oak island, light joinery and brass hardware from [Kaboodle's Brisbane farmhouse kitchen](https://kaboodle.com.au/inspiration-gallery/front-porch-properties-farmhouse-kitchen).
- Upholstery, soft silhouettes and timber furniture from [Jardan's Australian interiors](https://www.jardan.com.au/pages/furniture).
- Woven upholstery, loose cushions and the low sofa deck from [King Living's photographs of Olive Cooke's home in northern NSW](https://www.kingliving.com.au/blog/restorative-home-decor).
- Staggered hardwood and board-to-board colour variation from [Big River's Blackbutt flooring at Trojan House](https://www.bigrivergroup.com.au/project/trojan-house/).
- Muted olive laminate, pale worktops and brushed brass from [Laminex and MJ Harris Group's Marden Street kitchen photographs](https://www.laminex.com.au/article/Project_Feature_Marden_St).
- Flush enclosure finish and hardware from the [Clipsal Resi MAX product photo](https://agmelectrical.com.au/clipsal-schneider-special-order-in-only-products/plastic-resi-max/product-4873.html).
- Breaker dimensions, screw recesses, toggle profiles and terminal positions from the [Clipsal MAX9 slim RCBO product gallery](https://www.clipsal.com/products/circuit-protection/max9/residual-current-breaker-with-overcurrent-protection-rcbo-1pn-20a-30ma-c-curve-6000a-a-type-mx9r3120?itemno=MX9R3120) and [single-module instruction sheet](https://download.se.com/files?p_Doc_Ref=JYT3818100).
- Insulated comb, end caps and internal conductor dressing from [Clipsal's open MAX9 switchboard photos](https://www.clipsal.com/max9).
- Exposed framing and cable dressing from [Sydney residential electrical project photos](https://localelectricalsolutions.com.au/projects).

The project photographs informed the models and finishes. The scatter cushions use
original basket-weave colour maps. Other surfaces use the CC0 textures below.
